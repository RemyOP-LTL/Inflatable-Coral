A part is one pentagon plate and the spokes at its five corners (its arms), cut as one piece. Each arm hinges at its corner and folds along the dashed line. Every other spoke is loose or belongs to the spine.

The end of every spoke is a half circle of this width. Spoke length is measured between the centres of the two end half circles.

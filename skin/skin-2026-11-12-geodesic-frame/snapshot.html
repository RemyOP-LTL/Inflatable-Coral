Frame tool

Spokes with round ends, pentagons as solid plates, loose spokes riveted at both ends, spine along the inside of the curve.

Shape

Patch

A part is one pentagon plate and the spokes attached to it. Every other spoke is loose.

Plastic

The end of every spoke is a half circle of this width. Spoke length is measured between the centres of the two end half circles.

3D view

Reading the flat patch. Green = the part: one pentagon plate and the spokes attached to it. Orange = loose spokes that are cut on this part's sheet, each nested in a window; all loose spokes are one length. Every loose spoke has a number; the same number marks each place it rivets to (the spoke may belong to a neighbouring part's sheet). Purple = ends that rivet to the spine.

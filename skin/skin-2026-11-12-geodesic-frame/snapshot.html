Your question 1: the two ways to draw the pentagon

Same patch, same tube, same spokes. Only the pentagon plate differs. Left: the plate is the pentagon, with a hub circle on each corner. Right: a round hub, a disc just big enough to swallow the five corner circles, with the polyp hole in it. Spokes leave from the disc.

A. Pentagon outline
B. Round hub

Green = spokes and hubs that stay attached. Orange spokes end in a free circle. Red dashed line and number = how far (in) to pull that circle to the hub it belongs to, then rivet through the two small holes. Blue-ringed holes are hubs that will join the next patch.

What differs: B leaves an even rim all round the polyp hole (A has thin spots where the pentagon is a little irregular, the known issue from before). B also covers more of the neighbouring windows. The spoke positions, and so the pull distances, are the same in both.

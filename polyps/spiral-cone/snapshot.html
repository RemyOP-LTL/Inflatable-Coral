Please check

At these settings the 1 in fin is taller than the 0.5 in tip radius and about as tall as the tightest pitch, so the fins will crowd near the tip. A smaller ruffle width, a wider tip or a looser tip pitch fixes it.

  • Is the ruffle folded in half with both raw edges in the seam (1 in fin from a 3 in strip), or do you want a taller single layer?
  • Tip diameter, the two pitches and fabric width are my guesses.
  • Is a flared cuff acceptable? A straight cuff cannot be in the same flat piece without stretching.
  • Laser bed size? The ruffle strip is 40 in long and can be cut in pieces.
